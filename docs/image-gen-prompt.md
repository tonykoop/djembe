# Image-Gen Prompts

Concept image only; not fabrication authority. Do not include cut-ready dimensions, hole locations, or toolpaths.

## Hero Prompt

A single finished stave-built djembe standing upright as a real physical object, shown in a clean three-quarter product-photography angle, ~50mm lens. The drum has the unmistakable goblet silhouette: a wide rounded bowl at the top carrying a taut natural goatskin head, narrowing through a slender waisted neck, then flaring back out to an open foot that the drum rests on. The shell is built from vertical wooden staves rather than carved from one log — subtle vertical glue seams run head-to-foot down the lathe-turned hardwood body, mixing two warm North-American hardwood tones with a smooth progressive lacquer finish that catches the light. The head is held by a metal collar ring and rope-tuned with a mesh of pale double-braided polyester rope laced in the classic vertical-and-diamond Mali weave between a top crown ring and a lower ring, the verticals running taut down the bowl. The goatskin head shows faint natural grain and edge translucency near the rim. Realistic tabletop scale of a knee-height hand drum. Clean neutral seamless studio background, soft directional studio key light with gentle fill, high detail, photorealistic, no text labels.

## In-Context Prompt

The same stave-built goblet djembe staged in a warm woodworking shop or small drum studio, sitting on a plank workbench beside coils of pale tuning rope and a hardwood stave blank, late-afternoon natural window light raking across the lacquered shell to reveal the vertical stave seams and the rope lacing down the bowl. Shallow depth of field with the goblet body sharp and the bench tools softening into bokeh behind it, environmental storytelling of a craft-built drum mid-life in the shop, concept visualization only, photorealistic.

## Detail Prompt

Macro close-up of the djembe's signature rope-tuning system and stave seams: the metal flesh/crown ring seating the folded goatskin head edge, the vertical pull ropes of pale double-braided polyester running taut from the upper ring, and the diamond-knot tuning weave cinching them, with the lathe-turned hardwood bowl and its faint vertical stave glue lines filling the background. Raking side light emphasizes rope braid texture, the natural grain of the goatskin, the lacquer sheen, and the two-tone wood seams. High detail, shallow depth of field, no measurements.

## Negative / Avoid

No text, logos, watermarks, or fake maker marks; no dimension callouts, rulers, or tuning-number labels; no impossible mechanisms or floating rope; goblet proportions stay plausible; anatomically plausible hands if a player is shown.
