# Djembe Image-Gen Prompts

Concept visualization only; not fabrication authority. Do not infer goblet
geometry, jig dimensions, rope loads, head setup, or tuning from these prompts.

## Hero Prompt

Photorealistic 3/4 product photograph of a stave-built djembe with a goblet-shaped hardwood body: wide bowl, narrow neck, flared foot, visible vertical stave seams, lathe-smoothed exterior, natural goatskin head, metal rings, and braided rope tuning. The drum should look like a refined engineering-documentation object, not a mass-market import: locally sourced hardwood tones, signed-maker craft energy, clean rim work, and a bright articulate stave-built character. Clean studio background, soft directional lighting, realistic scale from a hand or beater nearby, 50mm lens, high detail, photorealistic.

## In-Context Prompt

Photorealistic scene of the stave-built djembe in a woodworking and acoustics workspace: one player sits with the drum tilted slightly forward for bass, tone, and slap testing, while nearby are scanned geometry notes, a tuner, rope tensioning tools, and a curved-stave or segmented-ring study on the bench. Natural light, believable hand posture, respect for the instrument's Mande origin without invented decoration.

## Detail / Macro Prompt

Photorealistic macro close-up of the djembe neck and head transition: natural goatskin folded over rings, braided rope tensioning, vertical stave seams curving into the goblet shoulder, and smooth lacquered hardwood grain. Show real knots, ring contact, shell curvature, and handmade surface detail. High-detail macro, shallow depth of field.

## Negative / Avoid

No text, logos, watermarks, impossible rope paths, fake tribal patterns, extra limbs, distorted hands, toy proportions, or cutaway dimensions.
